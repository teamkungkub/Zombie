# Nightfall visual system

The game uses the visual language of a quarantine field operations display. Its first surface is an Experience: a live, procedural 3D scene leads, with a quiet launch interface over it. NIGHTFALL and Sector 07 are fictional game setting choices.

The palette is charcoal green (#1b241d), off-white (#edf0e3), soft olive (#a9b1a0), pale green (#d0e3a7) for primary actions and amber for danger. Barlow Condensed carries the title and large HUD numbers; Geist is the utility face; Geist Mono is restricted to coordinates.

Use open composition, hairline separators, rectangular controls with 2px corners, restrained icon strokes, and Thai action labels. The scene continues behind the menu. The menu's left side owns launching and difficulty; the right side owns mission information. The bottom band joins the map and controls. Gameplay reduces the interface to the edges, leaving the aiming center clear.

Mobile stacks the launch surface and substitutes touch controls during gameplay. Pause and settings stop the simulation. Visible focus outlines and reduced-motion overrides are mandatory.
